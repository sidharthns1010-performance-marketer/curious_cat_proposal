02 • The Growth Strategy

4 Simple Changes That Will Unlock Profitable Sales

You already have great products, a loyal Indiranagar salon clientele, and viral Instagram Reels. Here is how we connect those pieces into a money-making machine.

01

Rebuild Shopify for Easy Buying

We will redesign your homepage and product pages on Shopify specifically for mobile shoppers (85%+ of Instagram users). Clear product benefits (like stopping whisker fatigue), customer reviews, sticky "Buy Now" buttons, and fast 1-step checkout will turn window shoppers into buyers.

02

Create Product Bundles (₹1,000 → ₹1,800+ AOV)

Selling a single ₹450 toy via ads rarely covers ad costs. We will group your bestsellers into curated "Mindful Cat Starter Kits" (e.g., Ceramic Bowl + Jute Toys + Guide) so customers happily spend ₹1,800–₹2,200 per order—making every ad click profitable.

03

Turn Best Instagram Reels Into Winning Ads

Your Instagram already has 926 posts and high-saving Reels. Instead of guessing what works, we will take your top-performing organic Reels, pair them with high-converting ad copy & clean offer posters, and run them through proper Meta Sales campaigns.

04

Bridge Salon Visitors & WhatsApp Automation

Your Indiranagar salon builds huge trust. We will run local salon ads alongside national product ads—and set up automated WhatsApp messages to recover abandoned carts, confirm COD orders, and recommend your ceramic bowls & toys to salon clients.
